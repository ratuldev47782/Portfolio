## Overview

Research project evaluating lightweight deep learning models for plant disease detection on mobile devices, with the
goal of making diagnosis possible in the field without server-side inference.

- **Paper**: *Advancing Plant Disease Detection with Deep Learning: Evaluating PlantNet and Transfer Learning Models
  for Mobile Applications* (2024)
- **Presented at**: IEEE International Conference on Quantum Photonics, Artificial Intelligence, and Networking
  (QPAIN 2025), BAUST, Bangladesh — Paper ID 214
- **Certificate**: [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1DRBpuBdorfKgqdAZyhxU0zJujx6OSJVL/view?usp=sharing)

## What I did

- Developed and evaluated **PlantNet**, a custom model based on **MobileNet V1**, for real-time plant disease
  detection through mobile applications.
- Benchmarked it against transfer learning and standard architectures — **CNN, InceptionV3, and VGG16**.
- Achieved **94.08% accuracy**, surpassing all benchmark models while staying small enough for on-device inference.

## Outcome

The published results show that a MobileNet V1 based architecture can outperform heavier transfer learning models for
this task, making real-time, on-device plant disease detection practical for mobile apps.
